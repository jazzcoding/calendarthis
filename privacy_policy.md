# Privacy Policy for CalendarThis!

**Last Updated:** June 15, 2024

## Introduction

Welcome to CalendarThis! This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use our mobile application ("App"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have a positive experience using our App.

## Information We Collect

### Information You Provide to Us

- **Event Data**: When you create events, we collect information such as event titles, descriptions, dates, times, locations, and attendee information.
- **User Preferences**: We collect your app preferences, such as language, date format, and notification settings.
- **API Keys**: If you choose to use AI features, we securely store your OpenRouter API key.

### Information We Collect Automatically

- **Device Information**: We collect information about your device, including device model, operating system version, and unique device identifiers.
- **Usage Data**: We collect information about how you use the App, including features you use and the time spent on different screens.
- **Images**: When you use the camera or image selection features, we process the images to extract text. Images are processed locally on your device.

### Information from Third Parties

- **Shared Text**: When you share text from other apps to CalendarThis!, we receive and process that text to extract event details.

## How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

- Provide, maintain, and improve the App's functionality
- Process and extract event details from text and images
- Create and manage calendar events
- Send notifications for upcoming events
- Customize your experience based on your preferences
- Analyze usage patterns to improve the App

## Data Storage and Security

### Local Storage

- **Event Data**: All event data is stored locally on your device using SQLite database.
- **Preferences**: Your preferences are stored locally using SharedPreferences.
- **API Keys**: API keys are stored securely using Flutter Secure Storage with encryption.

### Caching

- We use caching to improve app performance. Cached data is stored temporarily on your device and includes:
  - Event data
  - Text extraction results
  - API responses

### Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized or unlawful processing, accidental loss, destruction, or damage. However, please note that no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.

## Third-Party Services

### OpenRouter API

If you choose to use AI features, our App integrates with OpenRouter API to process text and extract event details. When you use these features:

- Text you submit is sent to OpenRouter's servers for processing
- We only send the text needed for event extraction
- We do not share your personal information with OpenRouter
- OpenRouter's use of the data is governed by their privacy policy, which you can find at [https://openrouter.ai/privacy](https://openrouter.ai/privacy)

### Image Processing

We process images locally on your device for text extraction. No images are sent to external servers.

## Permissions We Request

Our App requests the following permissions:

- **Camera**: To capture images for text extraction
- **Storage**: To access and save images for processing
- **Notifications**: To send event reminders
- **Internet**: To communicate with the OpenRouter API (if you choose to use AI features)

You can manage these permissions through your device settings. Denying certain permissions may limit some functionality of the App.

## Your Choices and Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal information:

- **Access**: You can access all your data directly in the App as it is stored locally on your device.
- **Correction**: You can edit any event or preference data directly in the App.
- **Deletion**: You can delete events or clear all data through the App settings.
- **Opt-out of AI Processing**: You can disable AI features in the settings if you prefer not to use them.

## Children's Privacy

Our App is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that we have collected personal information from a child under 13, we will promptly delete that information.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy in the App and updating the "Last Updated" date. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
